# SAMA Tone of Voice

## Core Tone
**Elegant, warm, intelligent, feminine, contemporary, confident.**

SAMA speaks like a knowledgeable friend who happens to have great taste — not a brand talking at you, not a salesperson pushing you, not a fashion magazine lecturing you.

## What SAMA Sounds Like
- Warm but not gushing
- Confident but not arrogant
- Elegant but not stiff
- Intelligent but not academic
- Feminine but not girlish
- Contemporary but not trendy-for-the-sake-of-it

## Caption Guidelines
- Short to medium length — say what needs to be said, then stop
- One clear idea per caption
- Maximum 2 emojis per caption
- End with one CTA or a question — not both
- No hashtag dumps in the caption (put hashtags in first comment)

## What to Flag and Rewrite

### Cheap-sounding sales language
❌ "Hurry! Limited stock! Order NOW before it's gone!"
✅ "Only a few pieces left — DM us to reserve yours."

### Excessive luxury jargon
❌ "Draped in the finest silks, this masterpiece transcends fashion..."
✅ "Silk-feel fabric that drapes beautifully and photographs even better."

### Overly dramatic captions
❌ "She walked in and the room fell silent. She was SAMA."
✅ "The kind of outfit that makes you walk a little differently."

### Generic motivational quotes
❌ "Dress for the life you want, not the life you have."
✅ (Skip the quote. Talk about the garment instead.)

### Clichés to avoid
- "Where elegance meets sophistication"
- "Redefining fashion"
- "Timeless elegance"
- "Effortlessly chic" (overused)
- "Luxury at its finest"
- "For the modern woman" (too generic)

### Emoji overuse
❌ "New collection drop! 🔥✨💫🌸👗 Shop now! 💕"
✅ "New collection — link in bio." (or one relevant emoji)
