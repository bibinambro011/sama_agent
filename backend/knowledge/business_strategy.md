# SAMA Business Strategy

## Core Business Principles
- Low to medium investment, small-batch testing before scaling
- Reusable patterns: multiple garments from similar fabrics and base patterns
- Efficient stitching: minimize waste, maximize output per tailor hour
- Limited dead stock: only produce what you can sell
- Strong margins: never compromise margin for volume at this scale

## Pricing Logic
**Cost components:** fabric + stitching + trims/embellishments + labour + packaging + overheads
**Margin:** minimum 40%, target 50–60% for hero products
**Perceived value uplift:** premium fabrics, signature details, and brand positioning can justify 10–20% above cost-based price

## Price Tiers
- **Entry price:** accessible, drives new customers, simple construction, good fabric
- **Hero price:** the collection's signature piece, best margin, most shareable
- **Premium price:** limited, elevated fabric/detail, positions the brand upward

## Inventory Strategy
- Test new designs in quantities of 3–5 first
- Repeat production only for proven sellers
- Made-to-order for premium and custom pieces
- Never overstock a new design

## Launch Strategy Phases
1. **Pre-launch (T-21 to T-7):** concept, design, fabric, sampling
2. **Teaser (T-7 to T-3):** mood content, fabric peeks, colour stories
3. **Preview (T-3 to T-1):** behind the scenes, design reveals, waitlist
4. **Launch (T-0):** hero products, reels, stories, ordering CTA
5. **Post-launch (T+1 to T+7):** styling content, customer reactions, best sellers, scarcity/restock

## Sales Channels
- Instagram (primary discovery and sales)
- WhatsApp (direct orders, repeat customers, personal service)
- Boutique walk-ins (experience, fittings, custom orders)
- Custom orders (premium, high-margin, relationship-building)
- Limited drops (creates urgency, reduces overstock risk)
- Pre-orders (validates demand before production)
- Bundles (increases average order value)
- Cross-selling (suggest matching bottoms, dupattas, accessories)
- Repeat customers (loyalty, early access, referrals)

## What to Test First
Prioritize designs that are: visually strong on Instagram, low-to-medium production complexity, use fabrics already in stock or easily sourced, have repeat-production potential, and appeal to the core customer.
