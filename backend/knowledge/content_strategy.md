# SAMA Content Strategy

## Content Pillars
Balance content across these pillars. Never make every post a sales post.

1. **Product** — showcasing garments, details, fabrics
2. **Education** — fabric care, styling tips, how to measure, what to wear when
3. **Storytelling** — the story behind a collection, a design, a fabric
4. **Behind the Scenes** — stitching process, fabric selection, fittings
5. **Founder Journey** — the designer's perspective, decisions, inspirations
6. **Fashion Inspiration** — mood boards, colour stories, trend interpretations
7. **Customer Transformation** — before/after, customer styling, testimonials
8. **Styling** — how to style one piece multiple ways, outfit combinations
9. **Sales** — ordering CTAs, limited availability, pre-orders, restocks

**Pillar balance rule:** Sales posts should not exceed 25% of total content. Aim for at least 4 different pillars per week.

## Reels Format
- Hook (first 2 seconds — must stop the scroll)
- Concept (what the reel is about)
- Shot-by-shot plan (specific, actionable)
- On-screen text (short, readable)
- Voiceover (optional but recommended for storytelling)
- Caption (tone-checked)
- CTA (one clear action)

## Photo Content Types
- Product shots (clean background, garment as hero)
- Lifestyle shots (real setting, real woman, real moment)
- Detail shots (close-up of the signature detail, fabric, embellishment)
- Fabric shots (texture, drape, colour in natural light)
- Before/after stitching
- Styling combinations (one garment, multiple looks)

## Stories Format
- Polls (engage, get feedback, drive decisions)
- Questions (invite conversation)
- Behind the scenes (process, fabric, stitching)
- Design voting (let customers choose between options)
- Countdowns (build anticipation)
- Testimonials (social proof)
- Ordering prompts (direct, not pushy)
