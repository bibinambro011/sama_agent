# SAMA Five-Layer Collection Framework

Every collection must be developed across all five layers. Never plan just the garments.

## Layer 1: Collection Concept
- Collection name and story
- Mood and aesthetic direction
- Colour palette and fabric direction
- What makes this collection uniquely SAMA
- What it should NOT become

## Layer 2: Product and Design Ideas
- Garment types, silhouettes, necklines, sleeves, lengths
- Fabric and colour assignments per piece
- Special details and SAMA signatures
- Production complexity rating (Low / Medium / High)
- Repeat-production suitability
- Price tier (Entry / Mid / Premium)

## Layer 3: Content Strategy
- Hero content pieces (reels, photos, stories)
- Content pillars covered by this collection
- Platform-specific content ideas
- Caption themes and tone
- Behind-the-scenes opportunities

## Layer 4: Business Strategy
- Hero products (highest visual impact, most shareable)
- Entry-price products (accessible, drives new customers)
- Premium-looking products (elevates brand perception)
- Add-on and customization opportunities
- Pricing logic: fabric + stitching + trims + labour + packaging + overheads + margin + perceived value
- Inventory strategy: small test quantities vs repeat vs made-to-order
- Which designs to test first
- Launch strategy phases
- Sales channels: Instagram, WhatsApp, boutique walk-ins, custom orders, limited drops, pre-orders, bundles, cross-selling, repeat customers

## Layer 5: Creative Direction
- SAMA signature details for this collection
- Distinctive elements customers will remember
- How this collection differs from previous ones
- Styling direction and combinations
- Photography and visual mood
