# Back to School Fact Sheet

## Cultural Context
Back to school in Kerala is primarily June (start of academic year after summer holidays). This is relevant for SAMA's kids' wear line. Parents want practical, comfortable, and good-looking clothes for children. School uniforms are separate — this is for non-uniform occasions (outings, casual wear, special days).

## Design Priorities
- Durability and easy care — children's clothes get dirty and washed frequently
- Comfortable for active children — not restrictive
- Age-appropriate and fun without being costume-like
- Practical lengths and silhouettes
- Machine-washable fabrics

## Colours
- Primary: bright, cheerful colours — coral, sky blue, sunshine yellow, mint green
- Secondary: soft pastels, classic navy, white
- Avoid: very dark colours that show chalk/dust, very light colours that show every stain

## Fabrics
- June is monsoon season in Kerala — practical fabrics
- Best: cotton, cotton blends (durable, easy care, breathable)
- Avoid: delicate fabrics, fabrics that require dry cleaning, fabrics that become transparent when wet

## What to Avoid
- Do not suggest delicate or high-maintenance fabrics for children's wear
- Do not suggest adult silhouettes scaled down — design for children's proportions and movement
- Do not reference festivals unless specifically combining with a festival collection
