# Winter Fact Sheet

## Cultural Context
"Winter" in Kerala (November–January) is mild by most standards — temperatures drop to 20–25°C in the plains, cooler in the hills. It is the most comfortable season for wearing slightly heavier fabrics. This is also peak wedding season and Christmas season. The content angle is the relief of cooler weather and the ability to wear richer fabrics.

## Design Priorities
- Slightly richer fabrics than summer — but still not heavy winter fabrics
- Layering opportunities (light jackets, overlays, dupattas)
- Deeper, richer colours that suit the cooler mood
- This is the season where slightly more structured garments work

## Colours
- Primary: deep jewel tones (emerald, burgundy, navy, deep purple), warm neutrals
- Secondary: mustard, rust, warm brown, forest green
- Metallics work well in this season
- Avoid: the very light pastels of summer — they feel out of season

## Fabrics
- Best: crepe, georgette, soft cotton-silk blends, chanderi, light velvet accents
- Acceptable: slightly heavier cotton, structured fabrics
- Avoid: full velvet garments (still too warm for Kerala), wool, heavy brocade

## What to Avoid
- Do not suggest heavy winter fabrics (wool, thick velvet) — Kerala winter is mild
- Do not suggest summer fabrics (linen, light cotton) as the primary choice
- Do not confuse with Christmas collection — winter is a season, Christmas is an occasion

## Timing
- November to January
- Overlaps with Christmas and wedding season
- Content can begin late October

## Content Angles
- "Finally, the weather to wear this" (relief of cooler weather)
- Richer fabrics, deeper colours — the winter edit
- Layering ideas for Kerala winter
- "From the office to a winter evening out"
- Styling: how to wear deeper colours
