# Valentine's Day / Date Night Fact Sheet

## Cultural Context
Valentine's Day (February 14) and date night dressing are relevant for SAMA's urban customer base. The aesthetic is romantic, feminine, and slightly elevated — not overtly sexy. Customers want to look special for a dinner, outing, or celebration without being overdressed.

## Design Priorities
- Romantic but tasteful — elegant, not revealing
- Feminine details: soft ruffles, delicate embroidery, subtle bows, flutter sleeves
- Comfortable enough for a dinner out (sitting, moving, eating)
- Photograph-friendly — this occasion generates a lot of photos

## Colours
- Primary: blush pink, dusty rose, deep red, burgundy, soft mauve
- Secondary: ivory, champagne, soft lavender
- Avoid: bright neons, heavy dark colours as primary

## Fabrics
- February in Kerala is warm — breathable but slightly elevated fabrics
- Appropriate: georgette, crepe, soft cotton-silk blends, light chiffon
- Avoid: heavy fabrics, stiff fabrics that don't move well

## What to Avoid
- Do not suggest overly revealing designs — SAMA's aesthetic is elegant, not sexy
- Do not suggest sarees or blouses
- Do not reference other festivals
- Keep it romantic but wearable — not costume-like

## Timing
- February 14
- Customers order 1–2 weeks before
- Content should begin first week of February

## Content Angles
- "Date night dressing — the SAMA way"
- Romantic colour story: blush, rose, and ivory
- "What to wear when you want to look effortlessly beautiful"
- Styling: one romantic piece, styled two ways
- Behind the scenes: delicate detail being stitched
