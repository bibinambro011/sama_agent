# Mother's Day Fact Sheet

## Cultural Context
Mother's Day (second Sunday of May) is a gifting and celebration occasion. For SAMA, this is both a gifting opportunity (daughters buying for mothers) and a self-purchase occasion (mothers treating themselves). The aesthetic is warm, elegant, and timeless — not trendy.

## Design Priorities
- Flattering for a range of ages (30s to 60s)
- Comfortable and wearable — not just beautiful
- Gifting angle: pieces that feel special and considered
- Mother-daughter matching or complementary outfits are a strong angle

## Colours
- Primary: soft florals, blush, sage green, warm ivory, soft lavender
- Secondary: dusty rose, soft coral, warm neutrals
- Avoid: very trendy or youthful colours that don't suit a range of ages

## Fabrics
- May in Kerala is hot — breathable fabrics essential
- Appropriate: cotton, linen, light georgette, soft cotton-silk blends
- Avoid: heavy fabrics

## What to Avoid
- Do not suggest overly youthful or trendy designs
- Do not suggest sarees or blouses
- Do not reference other festivals
- Avoid designs that only work on one body type — Mother's Day customer is diverse

## Timing
- Second Sunday of May
- Customers order / gift 1–2 weeks before
- Content should begin last week of April

## Content Angles
- "For the woman who makes everything beautiful"
- Mother-daughter matching outfits
- "Gift her something stitched for her" (custom stitching angle)
- Styling: timeless pieces that work across ages
- Behind the scenes: a piece being made as a gift
