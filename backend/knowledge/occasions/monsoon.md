# Monsoon Fact Sheet

## Cultural Context
Monsoon in Kerala (June–September) is heavy and prolonged. Customers still need to dress well for work, outings, and daily life. The practical challenges are: fabrics that dry quickly, colours that don't show water marks, lengths that don't drag in puddles, and fabrics that don't become transparent when wet.

## Design Priorities
- Quick-dry fabrics
- Darker or mid-tone colours that hide water marks and splashes
- Midi lengths (not floor-length — drags in rain)
- Easy-care and machine-washable where possible
- Avoid light colours that become transparent when wet

## Colours
- Primary: deep teals, navy, forest green, burgundy, deep purple, warm browns
- Secondary: mustard, rust, deep coral
- Avoid: white, cream, light pastels (show water marks, can become transparent)

## Fabrics
- Best: cotton, cotton blends, rayon (dries reasonably fast)
- Acceptable: crepe, georgette (dries fast, doesn't cling when wet)
- Avoid: linen (takes long to dry, wrinkles badly when wet), silk (water marks), velvet

## What to Avoid
- Do not suggest light or white fabrics
- Do not suggest floor-length garments
- Do not suggest fabrics that become transparent when wet
- Do not reference festivals unless they fall in monsoon season (Onam does — see onam.md)
