# Wedding Season Fact Sheet

## Cultural Context
Wedding season in Kerala typically peaks October–February and again in April–May. Weddings are multi-day events with different dress codes per function (engagement, mehendi, wedding ceremony, reception). Guests want to look elegant without outshining the bride. SAMA's role is guest wear and family wear — not bridal.

## Traditional Dress Norms
- SAMA does NOT make bridal wear — position clearly as guest and family wear
- Different functions have different formality levels
- Reception: most formal, evening wear, richer fabrics
- Wedding ceremony: elegant, traditional-leaning
- Engagement/mehendi: semi-formal, more relaxed
- Customers want versatile pieces they can rewear

## Colours
- Avoid: white and red (traditionally bridal colours in Hindu weddings), all-black
- Safe: jewel tones (emerald, royal blue, deep purple, burgundy), pastels, gold accents
- For Christian weddings: more flexibility with colours including pastels and soft tones
- Metallics (gold, silver, rose gold) as accents are always appropriate

## Fabrics
- Elevated fabrics appropriate: georgette, crepe, soft silk-look, chanderi, organza accents
- Must be comfortable for long events (4–6 hours of sitting, standing, dancing)
- Avoid: fabrics that crease badly, fabrics that are too stiff

## What to Avoid
- Do not suggest bridal wear or heavy lehengas
- Do not suggest garments that are too casual for a wedding
- Do not invent facts about fabric sourcing

## Timing
- Wedding season: October–February (peak), April–May (secondary)
- Customers order 2–4 weeks before the wedding
- Content is evergreen during wedding season months
