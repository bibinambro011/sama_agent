# Christmas Fact Sheet

## Cultural Context
Christmas in Kerala (and among the Christian community across South India) is a major family celebration. Church attendance, family gatherings, and festive meals are central. It is also widely celebrated socially across communities in Kerala. The aesthetic is warm, celebratory, and slightly more formal than everyday wear.

## Traditional Dress Norms
- Church wear: modest, elegant, slightly formal — not revealing
- Family gatherings: festive but comfortable
- Customers want to look special without being overdressed
- Western-influenced silhouettes are acceptable and popular

## Colours
- Primary: deep red, forest green, ivory, gold, burgundy
- Secondary: navy, emerald, dusty rose, champagne
- Festive metallics (gold, bronze) as accents are appropriate
- Avoid: orange, yellow, or colours associated with other festivals

## Fabrics
- December in Kerala is cooler (relatively) but still warm — light to medium weight fabrics
- Appropriate: georgette, crepe, soft silk-look fabrics, cotton-silk blends, velvet accents (not full velvet garments)
- Avoid: heavy winter fabrics (wool, thick velvet) — not suited to Kerala climate

## What to Avoid
- Do not reference Onam, Diwali, or other festivals
- Do not suggest sarees or blouses
- Avoid overly casual fabrics (plain cotton, linen) — Christmas calls for a slightly elevated feel
- Do not invent facts about sustainability or handloom

## Timing
- Christmas is December 25
- Customers start ordering stitching in late November / early December
- Content should begin first week of December
- Ordering deadline: December 10–12 for custom stitching
