# Summer Fact Sheet

## Cultural Context
Summer in Kerala (March–May) is hot and humid, with temperatures regularly above 35°C. Customers prioritise comfort and breathability above all else. This is not a festival season — it is a practical season. The content angle is comfort, freshness, and looking good despite the heat.

## Design Priorities
- Breathability is non-negotiable — fabric choice is the most important decision
- Loose, flowing silhouettes that allow air circulation
- Light colours that reflect heat
- Easy-care fabrics (sweat and washing resistant)
- Lengths that are practical in heat (midi rather than full-length)

## Colours
- Primary: whites, off-whites, light pastels (mint, blush, sky blue, pale yellow)
- Secondary: soft coral, light terracotta, sage green
- Avoid: dark colours that absorb heat, heavy prints that feel visually hot

## Fabrics
- Best: linen, cotton, cotton-linen blends, chambray, light rayon
- Acceptable: light georgette, soft viscose
- Avoid: polyester, synthetic blends, georgette that traps heat, velvet, brocade, anything heavy

## What to Avoid
- Do not suggest heavy fabrics under any circumstances
- Do not suggest dark colours as primary palette
- Do not suggest long sleeves without breathable fabric
- Do not reference festivals (this is a seasonal, not festival, collection)

## Timing
- March to May
- Content can begin late February
- No specific ordering deadline — ongoing season

## Content Angles
- "Staying cool and looking good — the summer edit"
- Fabric education: why linen works in Kerala heat
- "Light colours, light fabrics, light feeling"
- Styling: how to look put-together in the heat
- Behind the scenes: fabric selection for summer
