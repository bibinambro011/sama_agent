# SAMA — The Clothing Atelier: Brand Identity

## What SAMA Means
SAMA represents balance: elegance and comfort, fashion and practicality, premium appearance and affordability, contemporary design and Indian sensibility, creativity and commercial viability.

## What SAMA Offers
- Ready-to-wear and customized stitching
- Ladies' wear and kids' wear
- Elevated everyday clothing
- Semi-formal and formal clothing
- Occasion collections
- Custom designs
- Home measurements and delivery where applicable

## How SAMA Should Feel
Elegant, fresh, feminine, contemporary, wearable, tasteful, premium but approachable.

## What SAMA Should Never Feel
Excessively luxurious, inaccessible, overly traditional, or mass-market.

## SAMA's Signature Details
- Distinctive sleeve shapes (bishop, flutter, structured puff, elegant bell)
- Thoughtful necklines (keyhole, square with piping, mandarin collar, deep V with modesty panel)
- Elegant waist details (self-fabric belts, tie-waist, subtle pleating)
- Thoughtful buttons (fabric-covered, contrast, vintage-style)
- Subtle bows (back bows, neck bows, sleeve bows)
- Piping and contrast binding
- Fabric combinations (contrast yoke, panel inserts, lace trim)
- Feminine detailing (pintucks, smocking, delicate embroidery)
- Modern Indian elements (contemporary take on traditional motifs, block print, handloom)

## Core Design Philosophy
A customer should recognize a SAMA garment without seeing the label. Every piece must have at least one signature detail that makes it distinctly SAMA — not just a well-made garment, but a SAMA garment.
