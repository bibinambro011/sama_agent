# SAMA Collection Types

## 1. Elevated Casuals
**Tagline:** "Casuals but not ordinary."

**Purpose:** Comfortable, easy to wear, stylish without being over-designed.
**Occasions:** Work-from-home, office, outings, lunches, travel.

**Design approach:**
- Interesting necklines and elegant sleeves
- Small distinctive details that elevate a simple silhouette
- Good silhouettes that flatter without being tight
- Interesting fabric combinations
- Subtle embellishment — never overdone
- Modern Indian influences without being ethnic

**Fabrics:** Linen, cotton, rayon, viscose, cotton-linen blends
**Details:** Smart use of bows, buttons, piping, lace, embroidery, beads, pleats, panels, belts

---

## 2. Formality
**Tagline:** "Professional, polished and effortlessly elegant."

**Purpose:** Looks expensive without being expensive. Not boring office clothes.
**Occasions:** Office, meetings, presentations, business lunches, semi-formal, work-to-evening.

**Design approach:**
- Clean silhouettes with sophisticated necklines
- Structured details and refined sleeves
- Minimal embellishment — quality over decoration
- Premium-looking fabrics with strong tailoring
- Pieces that transition from day to evening

**Fabrics:** Crepe, georgette, structured cotton, poly-blend, silk-look fabrics
**Details:** Sharp collars, clean seams, subtle tucks, elegant buttons

---

## 3. Occasion Collections
**Purpose:** Special, feminine, memorable, photograph-friendly.
**Occasions:** Weddings, festivals, parties, date nights, celebrations.
**Festivals:** Onam, Diwali, Christmas, Eid, wedding season, birthdays, anniversaries.

**Design approach:**
- Elegant rather than flashy
- Match the specific occasion's cultural and aesthetic context
- Photograph-friendly — looks beautiful in pictures
- Wearable beyond the single occasion where possible
- Commercial practicality — not just beautiful but sellable

---

## 4. Seasonal Collections
**Summer:** Breathable, light fabrics, fresh colours, linen/cotton.
**Monsoon:** Practical, easy-care fabrics, suitable lengths, rain-friendly colours, low transparency and staining risk.
**Winter:** Layering, richer fabrics, deeper colours, jackets/overlays.
**Festive seasons:** Cultural relevance, modern silhouettes, wearability beyond the festival, commercial practicality.
